# Demo Video Script (3 to 5 Minutes)

**Target Duration:** ~4 minutes  
**Recommended Recording Tool:** Loom, QuickTime, or OBS (upload to Loom, Google Drive, or unlisted YouTube)  

---

### **Video Outline & Screen Cues**

| Timecode | Screen Action | Narration & Key Points |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:45** | **Overview & Document Ingestion**<br>• Show application home screen (`Analyze & Chat`).<br>• Open **Document Library**.<br>• Click **Upload Contract**.<br>• Attempt uploading a non-supported file (e.g. `.png` or `.txt`) to demonstrate strict rejection.<br>• Upload a contract PDF/DOCX.<br>• Highlight the 4 processing stages: `Uploading` → `Extracting text` → `Indexing clauses` → `Ready`. | *"Hello, welcome to the demonstration of LexQuery, our legal contract analysis web application. In Part A, we handle document ingestion for both PDF and DOCX formats. Here, our validation immediately rejects invalid file extensions. When uploading a contract, the user is never left wondering what is happening: our pipeline displays real-time status transitions as it extracts text, validates character density to detect unreadable scanned PDFs, and indexes clauses into SQLite."* |
| **0:45 – 1:45** | **Chat with Document & Agentic Research (Part C)**<br>• Select a contract.<br>• Ask: *"What is the limitation of liability cap and what claims are excluded?"*<br>• Observe the streaming tokens.<br>• Click the expandable **Agentic Research Process** badge.<br>• Click **Stop Generating** on a query to demonstrate that partial generation is safely preserved. | *"Next, we interact with the contract through chat. Notice that in accordance with Part C Option 2, the app does not dump raw text into the prompt. Instead, the model autonomously invokes tools: first `search_document` to find relevant sections, then `get_section` to fetch the specific clauses. All agent steps are streamed live. We also have a 'Stop' button that halts generation immediately while preserving whatever output was generated."* |
| **1:45 – 2:30** | **Deterministic Verified Quotes & Citation Highlighting**<br>• Point out the green **Verified Quote** badge under the response.<br>• Note the page number and document name.<br>• **Click the verified quote badge**.<br>• Watch the right pane: it automatically scrolls to the exact page and highlights the passage with an animated glowing yellow box.<br>• Point out that page/offsets were computed in code, not trusted from AI. | *"Here is the most critical requirement of the assignment: Verified Quotes. Every answer must be backed by exact quotes. Before showing this quote, our backend verification engine confirms that the text actually exists in the canonical document, allowing for whitespace and line-break variations. When I click this quote, the dual-mode viewer scrolls directly to the passage and applies an animated highlight overlay. It also gracefully handles multiline and page-crossing quotes."* |
| **2:30 – 3:30** | **Multi-Document Questions & Contract Comparison (Part B)**<br>• Switch to **Document Library** and check multiple contracts.<br>• Ask a comparative question across both.<br>• Show that each quote identifies its source contract and verifies against it.<br>• Switch to the **Compare Contracts** tab.<br>• Select Version A and Version B.<br>• Show the executive diff summary, filter by **High Significance**, and review the substantive legal shift cards. | *"In Part B, we support multi-document analysis: the user can select multiple contracts, and quotes are tagged and verified against each specific file. Next, our Contract Comparison tab aligns contracts at the clause level rather than doing a noisy character diff. It generates a plain-language summary of what changed in substance—distinguishing commercial shifts like liability caps from stylistic rewordings—and provides filters for High, Medium, and Low significance."* |
| **3:30 – 4:30** | **Large Document Strategy & Conclusion**<br>• Explain the 150-page strategy (hierarchical FTS5 + coverage accounting).<br>• Summarize Part C engineering hurdles (handling malformed tool calls, round caps).<br>• Conclude with local run setup and deployment. | *"For large 150-page contracts, our hierarchical FTS5 indexing and agent coverage tracker ensure the app never claims a clause does not exist unless all relevant sections were verified. The app runs locally with zero external database dependencies via SQLite and is production-ready for deployment. Thank you!"* |
